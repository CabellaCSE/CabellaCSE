- **Simple & Direct**: Keep all answers short, clear, and plain English.
- **No Coding Jargon**: Avoid technical terms, file names, or code snippets unless specifically asked. Gil (CFO) is a business owner and prefers direct business-focused explanations.

- **Future Vision**: Incremental expansion into custom inventory tracking, streamlined quoting, and accounting workflows over time.
