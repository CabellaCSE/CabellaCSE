Who We Are

Cabella Cabinets Stone & Flooring is a Las Vegas wholesale building-material supplier created to help contractors, builders, investors, property managers, and developers manage projects from product selection through pickup.

Our Story

Cabella opened in 2025 with a simple goal: give professional customers one dependable place to source ready-to-assemble cabinets, prefabricated stone countertops, flooring, sinks, hardware, and related materials.

Our showroom is not a traditional retail showroom. It is a working resource for our contractor customers. They can make appointments, bring homeowners to select materials, review layouts and renderings, and use the showroom as an extension of their own business.

Cabella does not provide installation services. We supply materials, coordinate purchasing and transportation, assist with measurements and renderings when requested, and help the contractor keep the project moving.

What We Sell

RTA cabinets, prefabricated stone countertops, flooring, sinks, cabinet hardware, butcher block, and related accessories.

Who We Serve

Contractors, builders, investors, property managers, developers, and other professional customers.

How We Work

Projects are managed from assignment and vendor quotes through estimates, payment, purchase orders, receiving, and documented customer pickup.

3. Learn these rules first

  1. Search CSE before asking a routine question.
  2. Projects are normally named by the jobsite address.
  3. Request vendor quotes individually.
  4. Do not order until the customer approves and pays.
  5. Create the PO from inside the correct QuickBooks Project.
  6. Follow the project until materials are received, matched, and documented.
  7. Photographs and the pickup signature are attached to the connected invoice.
